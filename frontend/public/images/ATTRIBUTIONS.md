Every entry below was visually checked against the actual pose it's meant to demonstrate before being included — several initial Commons candidates were rejected after inspection for showing the wrong pose or variant (e.g. a "Tadasana" photo with arms raised instead of at the sides, and a "Side Plank" photo that turned out to be a different pose entirely).
